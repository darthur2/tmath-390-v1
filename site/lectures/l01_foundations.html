Introduction to Statistics

What is Statistics?

Definition 1.1

The field of statistics is concerned with the scientific study of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and drawing conclusions from data.

Population

Definition 1.2

A population is the collection or set of all objects or measurements that are of interest to the collector.

Parameter

Definition 1.3

A parameter is any quantity of a statistical population that summarizes or describes an aspect of the population. These are often denoted as Greek letters or Latin letters without diacritics (e.g., \(\hat{}\), \(\tilde{}\), \(\bar{}\), etc.)

Some examples of population parameters are:

  • The population mean \(\mu\)
  • The population proportion \(p\)
  • The population variance \(\sigma^2\)
  • The population standard deviation \(\sigma\)

Sample

Definition 1.4

The sample is a subset of data selected from a population. The size of a sample is the number of elements in it.

Statistic

Definition 1.5

A statistic is any quantity computed from values in a sample which is considered for a statistical purpose. These are often denoted as Latin letters and sometimes include diacritics.

Some examples of sample statistics are:

  • The sample mean \(\bar{x}\)
  • The sample proportion \(\hat{p}\)
  • The sample variance \(s^2\)
  • The sample standard deviation \(s\)

Ex: Adult Heights

Example 1.1

A researcher wants to study the average height of all adult males in a city. She measures the height of 200 randomly selected adult males and computes the mean of these 200 heights.

The population is all adult males in the city. The sample is the 200 randomly selected adult males. The population parameter is the average height of all adult males in the city, \(\mu\). The sample statistic is the average height of the 200 random selected males, \(\bar{x}\).

Ex: Groceries

Example 1.2

A survey is conducted to find out the proportion of families in a specific town that spend more than $1000 a month on groceries. Data is collected from 150 randomly selected families and it is found that 60% of these families spend more than $1000 a month on groceries.

The population is all families in the specific town. The sample is the 150 randomly selected families in the town. The parameter is the proportion of all families in the town that spend more than $1000 a month on groceries, \(p\). The statistic is the proportion of the 150 families that spend more than $1000 a month on groceries, \(\hat{p}\).

Descriptive Statistics

Definition 1.6

Descriptive statistics are statistics that assist us in organizing, describing, summarizing, and displaying experimental or observational data.

Statistical Inference

Definition 1.7

A statistical inference is an estimate, a prediction, a decision, or a generalization about the population based on information contained in a sample.
